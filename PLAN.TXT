------------------------------------------------------------------

-> recognition -> structured JSON -> generic animation renderer ->
cartoon motion-graphics explanation with narration, zoom/pan camera
choreography, targeted at ADHD/low-concentration learners.

types, components, or scope in these final 5 days.
